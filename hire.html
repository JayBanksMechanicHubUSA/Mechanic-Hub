Technician levels

Tell us the level. We'll screen for it.

Every search is built around the level and requirements of your specific role.

MTop tier

Master Technicians

Highly experienced professionals with advanced diagnostic and repair capability. The people your other techs go to when a job has them stuck.

  • Advanced diagnostics across electrical, drivability and network faults
  • Deep experience on complex and repeat-comeback repairs
  • Often the hardest level to find, so searches can take longer
ASenior

A-Level Technicians

Highly skilled, experienced technicians who handle complex diagnostic work and a broad range of repairs.

  • Typically 7+ years of hands-on experience
  • Often a substantial personal tool investment, commonly $30,000 or more
  • Broad bumper-to-bumper repair capability
BCore

B-Level Technicians

Competent technicians who carry general repair and maintenance work and a defined range of diagnostics.

  • General repair and scheduled maintenance
  • Diagnostics within a defined scope
  • A solid fit for growing bays and high-volume shops

These are general indicators, not automatic qualifications. Every candidate is assessed on their actual experience, technical competence, diagnostic ability, tools and relevant certifications against the requirements of your specific role.

Process

How your search runs.

  1. Intake

    Understand the shop

    We learn your business, location, open role, technician level, pay range, experience, certification and tool expectations.

  2. Campaign

    Launch recruitment campaigns

    With your authorization, we run targeted recruitment campaigns through your own Meta ad account. You pay Meta directly for ad spend.

  3. Screening

    Source and screen candidates

    We review applicants against your requirements. Screening may cover work history, technical skills, diagnostic experience, certifications and tool ownership.

  4. Shortlist

    Present qualified candidates

    We introduce suitable candidates to you for further evaluation and interviews, with notes on how each matches your brief.

  5. Decision

    Support your hiring decision

    You interview and you decide. We coordinate between you and the candidate to keep the process moving.

  6. Guarantee

    30-day replacement guarantee

    If a placement doesn't work out in the first 30 days, we run a replacement search with no additional recruitment fee, under the written guarantee terms.

30-day replacement guarantee

If the hire doesn't work out, we search again.

If a technician placed through Mechanic Hub leaves or doesn't work out during the first 30 days, we'll run a replacement search with no additional recruitment fee, subject to the written terms of your service agreement.

What it covers

  • A new search for the same role
  • Sourcing, screening and a new shortlist
  • No second recruitment fee

What it doesn't mean

  • It's not a cash refund of the fee
  • Ad spend paid to Meta isn't refunded
  • A replacement isn't promised immediately
